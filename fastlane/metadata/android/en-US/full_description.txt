Photo Atlas aggregates and organizes your images and videos by time and space using their metadata.

Features:
- A planet map: every location with media becomes a highlighted section sized by the number of
  items. Sections merge when you zoom out and split when you zoom in, so two cities in the same
  country appear as one area far away and as two areas when you get closer.
- Tap a section to see the photos and videos it contains in a list right below the map.
- A vertical timeline: the last 2 months grouped by day, the previous 3 months grouped by week,
  everything older grouped by month.
- A full gallery that also shows items without metadata or with partial metadata.
- Scan local folders (Android and Linux) and index name, size, date, dimensions and GPS location.
- Connect your Infomaniak kDrive: choose a folder, scan it recursively and enrich dates and GPS
  from EXIF data. Images stay in your cloud; only metadata is indexed.
- Light and dark themes with a minimal color palette, English and Italian.

Photo Atlas is free and open source, with no ads and no tracking. The API can run on your own
machine with a local PostgreSQL database, and the same schema works on a Supabase free plan.
