Gallery selection and viewer reworked. Selection now starts with a long press only: drag over the
grid to select (or deselect) the items you pass, with automatic scrolling near the edges. Tapping an
item opens the full screen viewer: swipe left/right to change photo, scroll down for all the metadata
and a small map preview when the position is known, with Share, Select and Open in Maps in the menu.
Cloud photos are previewed at higher resolution in the viewer. The gallery is ordered by capture
date, newest first.
