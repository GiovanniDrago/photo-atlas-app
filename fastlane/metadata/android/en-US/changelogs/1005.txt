Fixed the NullPointerException that could abort the scan of a folder in background runs: the photo
permission is now checked in the foreground before a run starts (with a shortcut to the system
settings when it is missing) and background scans never ask for it, which is what crashed the
plugin without an Activity. Scans are also resilient: a page of the media library that cannot be
read is skipped and reported in the log instead of failing the whole folder.
