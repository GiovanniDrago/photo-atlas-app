Folder backups now run in a foreground service: starting the automatic upload of a folder keeps
going while you change screen, put the app in the background or close it, and a thin progress bar
above every screen shows the phase, the uploaded count, the queued folders and a stop button.
Interrupted runs release their pending items right away, so resuming retries them immediately
instead of waiting two hours, and the folder counters and gallery badges refresh as soon as a run
finishes. The folder switch no longer appears off after leaving the screen mid-upload.
