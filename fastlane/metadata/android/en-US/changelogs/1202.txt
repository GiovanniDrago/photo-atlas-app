The upload bar at the bottom is now tappable: while an upload or a failed-upload retry is running
it opens a live detail with the overall progress, the full list of files with their state
(completed, in progress, waiting, failed with the reason) and a Stop button that cancels after the
current file. At the end the sheet shows the summary, so you always know what was uploaded and what
failed. Works in the Gallery, in folder galleries and inside albums.
