Gallery rebuilt: indexed items and device files in one grid, with badges for the upload state
(cloud_done uploaded, cloud_queue not uploaded, cloud_off failed) and filters for photos, videos,
uploaded, not uploaded and missing metadata. Multi-select actions: upload (indexing device files
that are missing), share (system share sheet, cloud files are downloaded first) and delete with a
per-action dialog to remove the cloud copy (kDrive trash, recoverable) and/or the device file.
The gallery now loads as soon as it is opened and refreshes itself when the server address or the
session changes.
