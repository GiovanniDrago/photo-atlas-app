Deleting from the device now moves the files to the Android system trash (recoverable) on Android
11 and newer, with a permanent delete only on older versions. When the app is opened, returns to the
foreground or every 15 minutes, the folders enabled for automatic upload are scanned and the
missing files are uploaded right away (the 6-hour schedule only applies while the app is closed).
Fixed long uploads not starting on Android 14+: the dataSync foreground service is now declared, so
the progress banner starts instead of waiting forever (and offers a retry if a job does not start).
Pulling to refresh inside a folder now updates the uploaded counters too, and turning the automatic
upload off keeps the files already uploaded.
