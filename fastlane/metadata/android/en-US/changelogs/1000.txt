New Collections tab: the big card previews the newest photos and opens the timeline, and below it
every device folder is listed with an "Automatic upload" switch. Turning the switch on scans the
folder (without subfolders), indexes it and uploads everything it contains, with a progress bar; the
background job then keeps checking the same folders. Tapping a folder opens the gallery restricted
to that folder, with the same selection actions. The per-folder switches moved here from the Backup
screen, which keeps the schedule, manual runs and verification.
