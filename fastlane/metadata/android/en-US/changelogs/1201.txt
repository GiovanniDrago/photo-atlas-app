Album detail now handles uploads and deletions: select items and use Upload to retry pending or
failed uploads, and Delete to choose between removing them from the album, from the device (system
trash) or from kDrive (trash), individually or together. When an album has failed uploads the
header shows a red "failed uploads" row with a Retry button that re-uploads all of them in one run.
Device files of album items are matched on the fly, so the gallery badges and the device actions
work inside albums too.
