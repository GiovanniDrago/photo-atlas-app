Natural Earth data
==================

`ne_110m_land.geojson` is a low resolution land polygon dataset from Natural Earth.

- Source: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/
- Repository: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector
- License: public domain (Natural Earth terms: no permission needed, attribution appreciated but not required)

The data is used by `lib/widgets/planet_globe.dart` to render the orthographic planet view with a
minimal palette based on the active application theme.
